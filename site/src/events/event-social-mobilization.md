## Social Mobilization around Environmental Issues

## Time & Location

Sep 28, 2017, 12:00 PM

WORT 89.9FM or [wortfm.org](http://www.wortfm.org)

## About the event

Host Alan Ruff will be speaking with Pedro Cabeza, head organizer with CRIPDES, and with US El Salvador Sister Cities national leader Leigh Hardy, about social mobilization around environmental issues and the role of popular education in that work.
