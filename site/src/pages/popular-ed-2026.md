## Popular Education Workshop

**Sunday, October 5, 2026, 4:00–6:00 PM**
Village Co-Housing Community Room, 1104 Mound St, Madison
$10 suggested donation

Join us for a Popular Education workshop for community organizers, led by visiting Salvadoran social movement leaders.

Registration required: [info@mascp.com](mailto:info@mascp.com) or [mascp.org](/)

<h2 id="info">Info</h2>

### Solidarity to Strengthen the People's Power

**Description of the workshop:**

Power is built from the ground up, and it starts with our stories. Together, we will learn from each other's personal experiences in organizing, exchanging practical actions, triumphs, and hard-earned lessons that can be adapted and applied directly to our own home communities.

**Why Join Us?**

**Build Solidarity:** We will bridge diverse contexts to uncover the threads that connect us, reinforcing the truth that we are stronger together in the face of shared systemic issues.

**Cultivate cross-border community:** Resisting global struggles requires a global network. Here, you will find a supportive community committed to long-term collective resistance and mutual accompaniment.

Whether you are a seasoned organizer or just beginning to engage in community action, your voice and experience are essential. Let's learn, strategize, and build the power needed to create lasting change.

**Objectives:**

- Identify our common struggles, their causes and our allies to strategize and build power.
- Learn from each other about personal experiences in organizing, and consider how these practices might be applied in our own communities.
- Build solidarity in the face of common issues experienced across contexts.
- Build community to resist global struggles.
